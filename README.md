Entry for the [make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/u-eager-stork-81-9647-funniest-forecast-for-a-city-2a8769cf) bounty **"Funniest Forecast for a City That Isn't"**: one line, at most 120 characters, the funniest weather forecast for a city that does not exist.

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) writes the forecast to `/output/entry.txt`:

> Tonight in Puddleton: the moon will be out, the stars will be out, and so will your bins, in 60mph winds.
